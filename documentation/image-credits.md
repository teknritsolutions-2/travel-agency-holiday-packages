## Original identity and graphics

`logo.svg`, `logo-dark.svg`, `symbol.svg`, `dashboard-mark.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `brush.svg`, `horizon-panorama.svg` and `horizon-tall.svg` were created specifically for this project. They do not reproduce the identity or artwork of another travel agency. The Waylark mark uses a continuous geometric ribbon W. Light, dark, standalone, dashboard and favicon variants share the same geometry. All interface icons are inline SVG line drawings.

## Typography

Bricolage Grotesque and Outfit are served from local TTF files. Both use the SIL Open Font License. Full license texts are bundled beside the fonts in `assets/fonts/BricolageGrotesque-OFL.txt` and `assets/fonts/Outfit-OFL.txt`. Styles are declared in `assets/css/fonts.css`.

## Image use

The Home 1 and Home 2 heroes are distinct photographs of the Italian Dolomites. Domestic, international and honeymoon/family banners use different images. Destination labels refer to the photograph’s country or region, rather than claiming that a proposed package guarantees the pictured viewpoint or property. Resort photographs are destination inspiration; no named hotel or villa is promised.
